EVENTS AT E-SUMMIT’26

Architecting Impact

|

31 Jan – 1 Feb 2026

|

IIT Tirupati

Dive into a weekend of innovation, leadership, and entrepreneurship. From hands-on workshops and startup showcases to high-stakes pitch competitions and networking dinners, E-Summit '26 brings you a curated lineup of events designed to ignite your entrepreneurial spirit and architect the future.

DAY 01

Saturday, 31 January 2026

09:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Morning Sessions

Main Auditorium, Block A

  • Inauguration Ceremony: Lighting the lamp and opening address by the Director.
  • Keynote Address: "Future of Tech Entrepreneurship" by Industry Leaders.
  • Panel Discussion: Navigating the funding winter in 2026.
01:00 PM - 05:00 PM

Startup Expo

Academic Block Corridor

  • Showcase of 50+ innovative startups from across India.
  • Live product demonstrations and prototyping booths.
  • Networking opportunities with angel investors and VCs.
  • Student innovation corner featuring campus projects.
02:00 PM - 04:30 PM

Skill Workshops

Lecture Halls 1 & 2

  • Design Thinking: Problem-solving framework for founders.
  • Growth Hacking: Scaling from 0 to 100 users.
  • Interactive Q&A sessions with workshop mentors.

DAY 02

Sunday, 1 February 2026

09:30 AM - 01:00 PM

The Grand Pitch

Main Auditorium

  • Top 10 finalists pitch to a panel of VCs and angel investors.
  • Prize pool distribution of ₹5 Lakhs.
  • Live feedback and investment offers on stage.
02:00 PM - 03:30 PM

Leadership Talk

Open Air Theatre

  • Inspiring session with a renowned Unicorn Founder.
  • Topic: "Building Resilience in a Dynamic Market".
  • Interactive Q&A session with students.
04:00 PM - 06:00 PM

Closing Ceremony

Main Auditorium

  • Award distribution for Pitch Competition and Hackathon winners.
  • Vote of thanks by E-Summit '26 Organizers.
  • Cultural performance and networking tea.
